      pdf.keeplocal.tools

      Compress a PDF without uploading it.

      Re-encode supported embedded images and remove optional metadata in the local Rust core. When no reduction is possible your original PDF comes back exactly as it was — metadata included — and the result says so. The PDF stays on this device from start to finish.

      Compression settings

      Image quality

      Baseline JPEG images are recompressed. JPEG 2000, fax, and other image formats are preserved unchanged to protect compatibility.
